# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by user: portable static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, esbuild for vendored dependencies, browser-local demo persistence. User chose code-first design.
## Users
School owner/corporation members evaluating P2 Digital Works' pitch. Demonstration roles represent administrators, registrars, teachers, cashiers and gate staff.
## Product Purpose
Demonstrate a public school website and a connected school management portal using explicitly fictional data.
## Operating Context
A presentation and hands-on browser demo, not a production school system. All changes stay in this browser. No real student or financial data is permitted.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Public home, about, academics, admissions, news, contact. Explicit demo entry, overview, create/update/archive fictional students, grades with editable demonstration weights and review state, payment recording and balanced journal, campus QR entry/exit, reporting CSV and printable internal forms. Official LIS forms are outside scope. No actual payments or admissions submissions. No server authentication. Gate records do not establish class attendance.
## Brand Commitments
Star of the Sea High School, Inc.; P2 Digital Works as developer. Proposed deep navy, sea blue, restrained gold, marked provisional. Newsreader and Source Sans 3 as prior proposed typography. No official logo, endorsement, faculty, statistics, mission, tuition or current academic strands may be invented.
## Evidence on Hand
DepEd public ESC and SHS lists identify school 404868 in Tukuran, Zamboanga del Sur. Sources in docs/FACTS.md. Authorized school photos, logo, contact details and current policies have not been supplied.
## Product Principles
- One connected fictional dataset proves the workflow.
- Every surface makes demo status clear.
- Unverified school content remains visibly pending approval.
- Keep data exportable and the source portable.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive public and admin surfaces; keyboard operation, visible focus, labeled forms, accessible feedback, reduced motion. English; PHP currency; Asia/Manila timestamps.
